    Who is Contrast Mechanical?

    Contrast Mechanical, Inc. is a family-led commercial mechanical construction contractor in Macomb, Michigan. Doug Bowers, a veteran master plumber, founded the company in Detroit in 1996.

    Where is Contrast Mechanical located?

    Contrast Mechanical's offices are at 24546 21 Mile Road, Macomb, MI 48042. The company moved its corporate offices to Macomb in 2009 after earlier offices in Detroit and Ferndale. Call (586) 786-1200 or email info@contrastmechanical.com.

    What services does Contrast Mechanical provide?

    Contrast Mechanical provides commercial plumbing, pipe fitting, certified welding, mechanical insulation and HVAC, including sheet metal, refrigeration and air conditioning, as well as controls, balancing and design/build work.

    Are Contrast Mechanical's plumbers licensed?

    Yes. Contrast Mechanical employs licensed plumbers and master plumbers with a keen understanding of current plumbing codes, and its plumbers have more than 150 combined years of experience.

    Are Contrast Mechanical's welders certified?

    Yes. All of Contrast Mechanical's welders are certified in 7018 and 6010 position welding and have years of experience in the commercial industry.

    What ongoing service and maintenance does Contrast Mechanical offer?

    Contrast Mechanical offers backflow certification and repair, CSD-1 boiler testing, boiler installation and service, seasonal start-ups, shut-downs and preventative inspections, geothermal systems, valve inspection and repair, energy audits and insulation upgrades, and custom planned maintenance agreements.

    What projects has Contrast Mechanical worked on?

    Contrast Mechanical's projects include Bloomfield Hills High School, its largest to date at a $13 million mechanical contract, along with Harper Woods High School, Marysville High School, Jimmy John's Field, Royal Oak Lofts and Shelby Crossing Health Campus.

    Who leads Contrast Mechanical?

    Doug Bowers is CEO and founder, Paul Bowers is President, Lead Estimator and Senior Project Manager, and Rebecca Bowers is Vice President. Nathan Marotta is Director, Todd Kobel is Operations Manager, and Dave Carr is Field Supervisor.
