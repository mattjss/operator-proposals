Our story

Started as a small shop in Erie. Still family.

H. Jack's began as a small, local shop in Erie, Pennsylvania. More than 45 years later it is still family-owned and family-operated, with five shops serving Northeast Ohio, Northwest Pennsylvania and Western New York, open every day of the year.

The crew grew from plumbers into a full home team: HVAC techs, carpenters, tilers, drywallers and remodelers, all in-house. The giving grew with it, from the Greater Cleveland Food Bank to Friday night football.

Questions

Before you call.

Quick answers about our shops, phone lines, pricing and financing.

Who is H. Jack's Plumbing & Heating?

H. Jack's Plumbing & Heating Co. is a family-owned, family-operated plumbing, heating, cooling and remodeling company that started as a small local shop in Erie, Pennsylvania, and has served customers for more than 45 years.

Where does H. Jack's work?

H. Jack's serves Northeast Ohio, Northwest Pennsylvania and Western New York from five shops: Wickliffe, Cleveland and Akron in Ohio, Erie in Pennsylvania, and Jamestown in New York.

What numbers do I call?

Call 888.458.1161 in Ohio, 814.838.2239 in Pennsylvania, or 716.664.5809 in New York.

Is H. Jack's available for emergencies?

Yes. H. Jack's takes emergency calls 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.

How does H. Jack's price a job?

H. Jack's gives an honest, upfront quote once a technician has seen and assessed the problem. Estimates are free on plumbing and new HVAC installations, and the company stands by its work 100%.

What services does H. Jack's offer?

Plumbing (drain cleaning, sewer service, water heaters including tankless, gas and water lines, backflow prevention, basement waterproofing, excavation), HVAC (furnaces, radiators, boilers, ductwork, air conditioning), and remodeling (kitchens, bathrooms, tubs and showers, basements, tile and drywall) for homes and commercial systems.

Does H. Jack's offer financing?

Yes. H. Jack's offers financing for most plumbing, heating, air conditioning and remodeling projects. Approval is done over the phone and usually takes no more than 10 minutes.

What payment does H. Jack's accept?

H. Jack's accepts Visa, Mastercard, Discover and American Express, as well as financing.

Is H. Jack's involved in the community?

Yes. H. Jack's has supported the Greater Cleveland Food Bank since 2019, sponsors Fox 8's Friday Night Touchdown Player of the Week, and received the 2024 Medical Mutual Pillar Award for Community Service. The company was also featured on HGTV's Celebrity IOU.
