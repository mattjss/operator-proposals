What the crew does

Plumbing, heat, cooling, septic and water. All from one shop.

Installation, maintenance and repair for homes, farms, B&Bs, businesses and new builds. The 4,900 sq ft heated building in Lanesboro is stocked with a complete inventory of plumbing and heating products.

  1. Plumbing

    Protecting your home, your water and your peace of mind.

    • Leaks, broken pipes and clogged drains
    • Tank and tankless water heaters
    • Sinks, faucets, toilets, showers and tubs
    • Kitchen and bath remodels
    • Code-compliant installs for homes and businesses
    • Drain and fixture maintenance

    Jon Willford has held a Minnesota Master Plumber license since 1991, and is also licensed in Iowa. Fixtures from Kohler, Moen and Delta.

  2. Heating

    The area's heating experts for decades: installation, maintenance and repair.

    • Gas furnaces (Lennox)
    • Oil furnaces
    • Boilers and in-floor heat
    • Heat pumps
    • Geothermal
    • Electric thermal off-peak storage
    • Electric heat
    • Fireplaces

    Electric thermal storage heats on off-peak, dual-fuel rates. Ask how it fits your home or farm.

  3. Air conditioning

    Sized and installed correctly the first time.

    • Central air
    • Heat pumps
    • Ductless mini-splits
    • Maintenance and repair

    Mini-splits need no ductwork, which makes them a good fit for remodels, additions and guest rooms. Lennox and Mitsubishi Electric.

  4. Septic systems

    On-site sewage treatment systems for homes and farms without city sewer.

    Certified by the Minnesota Pollution Control Agency in five categories:

    • Inspector
    • Designer I
    • Designer II
    • Installer
    • Pipe Layer

    RootX root control (2 lb or 4 lb) and BioClean drain cleaner are available from the shop.

  5. Water

    Softeners and drinking water for hard, iron-heavy water.

    • Water softener install and service
    • Hard water and iron problems
    • Scale prevention
    • Reverse osmosis drinking water

    Water-Right softeners and systems.

Also on the route

  • Commercial HVACInstalls, service and repair for local businesses, with preventative maintenance programs.
  • New construction, remodels and additionsPlumbing and heating planned with your builder. Call before you start your project.
  • Indoor air qualityFiltration, purification, odor removal and humidity balance, plus filters and parts.
  • Maintenance agreementsLonger-lasting equipment, fewer repairs and warranty compliance.

Questions

Good to know before you call.

Still wondering? The office answers Monday to Friday, 7:30am to 5pm.

Where is VIS Plumbing & Heating located?

VIS Plumbing & Heating is based at 105 Sheridan Street in Lanesboro, Minnesota, and lists locations in Lanesboro, Rushford and Harmony. Call 507-467-2240 for Lanesboro and Rushford or 507-886-2008 for Harmony.

How long has VIS Plumbing & Heating been in business?

Rolland and Irene Vis started the business in 1965 in the basement of their house in Lanesboro, Minnesota. Jon and Barb Willford bought it in 1988, and their son Michael joined full time in 2000. It is family owned and operated.

What areas does VIS Plumbing & Heating serve?

VIS Plumbing & Heating works with residential, commercial and farm customers throughout Southeast Minnesota and Northeast Iowa.

What are the business hours?

The office is open Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM.

Is VIS Plumbing & Heating licensed?

Yes. Jon Willford received his Master Plumber license from the State of Minnesota in 1991 and later also in Iowa. The company is certified by the Minnesota Pollution Control Agency for septic work as Inspector, Designer I, Designer II, Installer and Pipe Layer.

What services does VIS Plumbing & Heating offer?

Plumbing, water heaters, water softeners and reverse osmosis, kitchen and bath fixtures, septic systems, heating (gas, oil and electric furnaces, boilers, in-floor heat, fireplaces, geothermal, electric thermal storage, heat pumps), air conditioning and mini-splits, commercial HVAC, indoor air quality, maintenance agreements, and plumbing and heating for new construction, remodels and additions.

What brands does VIS Plumbing & Heating install?

VIS Plumbing & Heating is a Lennox dealer and also works with Kohler, Moen, Delta, Dunkirk, Burnham, Mitsubishi Electric, Unico System, GeoStar, ThermGeo, Navien, Bradford White and Water-Right.

Does VIS Plumbing & Heating offer financing?

Yes. VIS Plumbing & Heating works with the Service Finance Company for financing, and also suggests checking with your own bank first.

What should I do if I smell gas?

If you smell gas, call 911 and get out of the building.
