Questions

Good to know.

Where is VIS Plumbing & Heating located?

VIS Plumbing & Heating is based at 105 Sheridan Street in Lanesboro, Minnesota, and lists locations in Lanesboro, Rushford and Harmony. Call 507-467-2240 for Lanesboro and Rushford or 507-886-2008 for Harmony.

How long has VIS Plumbing & Heating been in business?

Rolland and Irene Vis started the business in 1965 in the basement of their house in Lanesboro, Minnesota. Jon and Barb Willford bought it in 1988, and their son Michael joined full time in 2000. It is family owned and operated.

What areas does VIS Plumbing & Heating serve?

VIS Plumbing & Heating works with residential, commercial and farm customers throughout Southeast Minnesota and Northeast Iowa.

What are the business hours?

The office is open Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM.

Is VIS Plumbing & Heating licensed?

Yes. Jon Willford received his Master Plumber license from the State of Minnesota in 1991 and later also in Iowa. The company is certified by the Minnesota Pollution Control Agency for septic work as Inspector, Designer I, Designer II, Installer and Pipe Layer.

What services does VIS Plumbing & Heating offer?

Plumbing, water heaters, water softeners and reverse osmosis, kitchen and bath fixtures, septic systems, heating (gas, oil and electric furnaces, boilers, in-floor heat, fireplaces, geothermal, electric thermal storage, heat pumps), air conditioning and mini-splits, commercial HVAC, indoor air quality, maintenance agreements, and plumbing and heating for new construction, remodels and additions.

What brands does VIS Plumbing & Heating install?

VIS Plumbing & Heating is a Lennox dealer and also works with Kohler, Moen, Delta, Dunkirk, Burnham, Mitsubishi Electric, Unico System, GeoStar, ThermGeo, Navien, Bradford White and Water-Right.

Does VIS Plumbing & Heating offer financing?

Yes. VIS Plumbing & Heating works with the Service Finance Company for financing, and also suggests checking with your own bank first.

What should I do if I smell gas?

If you smell gas, call 911 and get out of the building.
