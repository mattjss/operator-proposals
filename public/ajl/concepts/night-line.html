FAQ

Quick answers

Where is AJL Plumbing & Heating located?

AJL Plumbing & Heating is a locally owned and operated plumbing and heating company based in Oneonta, New York 13820. It serves Oneonta, Cooperstown, Cobleskill and Otsego County.

Does AJL Plumbing & Heating offer emergency service?

Yes. AJL Plumbing & Heating provides 24/7 emergency service. Call the emergency line at 607-434-0453.

What are AJL Plumbing & Heating's office hours?

The office is open Monday through Sunday, 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM. The office number is 607-878-9022.

Is AJL Plumbing & Heating licensed and insured?

Yes. AJL Plumbing & Heating is licensed and insured.

What services does AJL Plumbing & Heating offer?

Residential and commercial plumbing and heating: boiler installation and repair, hot water heaters, new plumbing fixtures, water filtration, garbage disposals, drain cleaning, toilet repair and installation, sump and well pumps, gas lines and gas leak detection, trenchless sewer repair, HVAC, radiant floor heating and oil-to-gas conversions.

Does AJL Plumbing & Heating work on commercial buildings?

Yes. AJL Plumbing & Heating serves restaurants, hotels and resorts, offices, clinics and hospitals, nursing homes, arenas, shopping centers, grocery and convenience stores, food processing plants, schools and colleges, and public buildings and airports, including commercial drain cleaning and sewer services.

Can AJL Plumbing & Heating convert my home from oil to natural gas?

Yes. AJL Plumbing & Heating performs oil-to-gas conversions and installs gas lines, along with boiler, radiant heating and HVAC work.

What water treatment systems does AJL Plumbing & Heating install?

AJL Plumbing & Heating installs sediment filters, reverse osmosis systems, salt-based water softeners and conditioners, and mixed-media whole-house filtration systems for city and well water.

How fast will AJL Plumbing & Heating respond to a service request?

Customers who complete the online service estimate request are contacted within the hour.
