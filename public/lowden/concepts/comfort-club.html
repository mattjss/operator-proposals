Heating & cooling

Furnace, AC and everything in between.

Repairs, replacements and new installs for existing homes and new construction, starting with an in-home consultation and estimate.

  • AC installation

    Central air, ductless mini-splits, geothermal cooling, hybrid systems, even portable and window units. A typical install or replacement takes 4 to 8 hours. Estimates are free.

    • Free estimates
    • 4–8 hour typical install

  • AC repair

    Warm air, odd noises or an AC that won't start. We repair existing systems no matter who made them.

  • Furnaces & preventive maintenance

    New units for existing homes and new builds, plus the yearly tune-ups that keep a furnace safe through an Iowa winter.

    • $250 off a new furnace

  • Ductless mini-splits

    • Lennox
    • Daikin

    • Fujitsu

    Uses the earth's steady underground temperature instead of outside air. Horizontal, vertical and pond or lake loops.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Something else? Call Cedar Rapids at (319) 899-4381 or Lowden at (563) 941-7701.

Where is Lowden Plumbing & Heating located?

Lowden Plumbing & Heating is a locally owned plumbing and HVAC company with two offices in Eastern Iowa: 616 Main St in Lowden, Iowa, and 1601 Ellis Blvd. NW in Cedar Rapids, Iowa.

How long has Lowden Plumbing & Heating been in business?

Lowden Plumbing & Heating has served Eastern Iowa since 1953.

What areas does Lowden Plumbing & Heating serve?

Lowden Plumbing & Heating serves Linn, Cedar, Johnson, Scott and Clinton Counties, including Cedar Rapids, Marion, Hiawatha, Lowden, Mechanicsville, Tipton, Clarence, Stanwood, DeWitt, Grand Mound, Wheatland, Mt. Vernon, Lisbon, Solon, Anamosa and nearby towns.

How do I reach Lowden Plumbing & Heating?

Call the Cedar Rapids office at (319) 899-4381 or the Lowden office at (563) 941-7701, or send a service request through the website form. The company does its best to respond within 24 hours.

Does Lowden Plumbing & Heating work on farms and wells?

Yes. Lowden Plumbing & Heating drills and inspects wells, installs and repairs well pumps and pressure tanks, handles well abandonment and water quality testing, and plumbs agricultural water systems and livestock heating and cooling.

What heating and cooling services does Lowden offer?

Lowden Plumbing & Heating installs and repairs central air conditioning, ductless mini-splits and geothermal systems, performs preventative maintenance, and services existing HVAC units regardless of manufacturer. It is a Lennox Premier dealer.

Is Lowden Plumbing & Heating licensed?

Yes. Lowden Plumbing & Heating lists license numbers C145147 and 52914-CL and states that its technicians are licensed and insured.

Does Lowden offer a maintenance plan?

Yes. The Complete Home Protection Program is an annual maintenance plan at $199 per year for one system or $299 per year for two, with annual heating and cooling inspections, 10% off parts and priority service within 3 days.

Does Lowden offer financing?

Yes. Lowden Plumbing & Heating offers financing through GoodLeap, which uses a soft credit check until funding.
