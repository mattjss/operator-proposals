Where is Willenborg Plumbing & Heating located?

Willenborg Plumbing & Heating, Inc. is a locally owned, family-run plumbing company at 1010 Cedar Cross Road in Dubuque, Iowa. It serves Dubuque, Peosta and the surrounding area.

What are Willenborg's hours?

The office is open Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM, and closed on weekends. Call (563) 556-2134.

Does Willenborg give free estimates?

Yes. You can request a free estimate for any plumbing service, including remodels and new construction.

What plumbing services does Willenborg offer?

New construction plumbing, remodeling and rerouting, pipe and drain repair with pressure jet and electric drain cleaning, sump pump installation, water heater installation and repair, tankless water heaters, fixture and toilet repairs, sink installs, garbage disposals and plumbing inspections.

Does Willenborg work on commercial buildings?

Yes. Willenborg works for residential and commercial clients, including plumbing for new business facilities and renovations of restaurants and offices.

Is Willenborg backflow certified?

Yes. Willenborg's plumbers are backflow certified.

Do I need a sump pump in my Dubuque basement?

If your basement is prone to water, a sump pump is the first line of defense against a flood. Iowa gets a lot of rain in spring and summer, and Willenborg installs a drain pipe along the edge of the basement floor that carries water to a sump pit, where the pump sends it outside automatically.

When should I call a plumber about my water heater?

Call if it makes strange noises, stops heating water properly, or produces rusty water. Willenborg repairs and replaces all brands and types, tank and tankless.
