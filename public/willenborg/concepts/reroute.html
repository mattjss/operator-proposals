Remodeling

Three reasons to reroute.

A new layout only works if the water goes where the fixtures go. These are the remodels Willenborg plumbs most often, and they all start with a free estimate.

  1. 01

    Move the kitchen sink or dishwasher

    Want the sink in the island, or the dishwasher on the other side of the room? The supply and drain lines move with them, so the new layout works the way you drew it.

  2. 02

    Move the toilet

    A cramped bathroom often comes down to where the toilet sits. Moving it can open up the whole room for a bigger vanity or a better shower.

  3. 03

    Upgrade the original plumbing

    Renovating an older bathroom? With the walls and floor already open, it is the right time to replace old pipes and fixtures instead of building over them.

    Do I need a sump pump in my Dubuque basement?

    If your basement is prone to water, a sump pump is the first line of defense against a flood. Iowa gets a lot of rain in spring and summer, and Willenborg installs a drain pipe along the edge of the basement floor that carries water to a sump pit, where the pump sends it outside automatically.

    When should I call a plumber about my water heater?

    Call if it makes strange noises, stops heating water properly, or produces rusty water. Willenborg repairs and replaces all brands and types, tank and tankless.
