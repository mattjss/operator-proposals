    Where is Complete Comfort Heating, Cooling & Refrigeration located?

    Complete Comfort Heating, Cooling & Refrigeration is at 1180 Shellsford Road in McMinnville, Tennessee 37110. It serves Warren County and the surrounding counties. Call 931-743-9878.

    Does Complete Comfort work on homes and businesses?

    Yes. Complete Comfort handles residential and commercial heating and cooling, and commercial refrigeration.

    What heating and cooling equipment does Complete Comfort install?

    Complete Comfort installs, sells and repairs heat pumps, natural gas units and indoor air purification systems. It is an American Standard authorized dealer, and American Standard offers up to a 12 year warranty on select models.

    Does Complete Comfort repair commercial coolers and freezers?

    Yes. Complete Comfort offers 24/7 service on commercial refrigeration of all brands, including restaurants, convenience stores and schools, and gives free estimates on repair or replacement units.

    Is Complete Comfort available 24/7?

    Yes. Complete Comfort offers 24/7 service with certified service technicians for unexpected equipment failure.

    Are Complete Comfort's technicians certified?

    Yes. Complete Comfort Heating & Cooling is EPA certified and NATE certified.

    Does Complete Comfort give free estimates?

    Yes. Complete Comfort offers free estimates and consultations on new systems and on repair or replacement units.

    Does Complete Comfort offer financing?

    Yes. Complete Comfort offers financing year round. Contact the company at 931-743-9878 for current offers.
