    Cold-weather work

    When a northern Illinois winter gets into the pipes

    The four calls that come with a hard freeze, and what Rowe's does for each.

    1. The sewer line froze solid

      When a hard freeze turns the sewer line to ice, nothing in the house drains. Rowe's uses hot water jetting to unclog frozen sewer lines, and repairs the line if it needs it.

      • Hot water jetting
      • Sewer line repair
      • Water and sewer line location
      Request a call back for this

    2. A pipe froze, or split

      Rowe's repairs frozen and leaky pipes and replaces water pipe where it's needed, including sewer pipe.

      • Frozen or leaky pipe repair
      • Water pipe replacement
      Request a call back for this

    3. There's a leak and you can't see where

      Rowe's finds and fixes water leaks, and can locate buried water and sewer lines before anyone digs.

      • Water leak detection and repair
      • Water and sewer line location
      Request a call back for this

    4. The sump pump quit, or the basement is backing up

      Rowe's replaces sump pumps and adds backup systems, installs floor drains, and fits back-flow prevention.
