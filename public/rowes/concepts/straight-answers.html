06 FAQ

Straight answers to fair questions

Don't see yours? Call (815) 821-4540 and ask.

Where is Rowe's Plumbing & Heating located?

Rowe's Plumbing & Heating is a locally owned plumbing company in Freeport, Illinois. Call (815) 821-4540.

How long has Rowe's Plumbing & Heating been in business?

Rowe's Plumbing & Heating has over 23 years of experience in the plumbing business.

Is Rowe's Plumbing & Heating licensed?

Yes. Rowe's Plumbing & Heating holds license number 058-168170.

Does Rowe's Plumbing & Heating give free estimates?

Yes. Call (815) 821-4540 for a free estimate on plumbing, sewer, water heater or water softener work.

What plumbing services does Rowe's offer?

Plumbing inspections and tune-ups, sink and toilet repairs, frozen or leaky pipe repairs, gas piping, sink, bathtub and shower installation, sump pump replacement and backup systems, garbage disposal repair and replacement, and shower pan repair.

Can Rowe's clear a frozen sewer line?

Yes. Rowe's Plumbing & Heating uses hot water jetting to unclog frozen sewer lines, and also handles sewer line repair, drain and rooter line cleaning, leak detection, backflow prevention and water and sewer line location.

Does Rowe's install water heaters and water softeners?

Yes. Rowe's installs, replaces and repairs tankless and traditional water heaters, and installs and replaces water softeners, conditioning and filtration equipment. Brands include A.O. Smith water heaters and International and Sterling water softeners.

What warranty does Rowe's Plumbing & Heating offer?

Drain cleaning carries a 1-month warranty, and Rowe's provides a 1-year warranty on most of its products and services.

How quickly can Rowe's finish a job?

Small jobs can usually be completed the same day. Bigger jobs take about two days.
