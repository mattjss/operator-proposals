The 29-point inspection

Exactly what we check, point by point.

Your technician works through the same list on every visit, on the outdoor unit and the indoor system. Points marked "where applicable" depend on the equipment you have.

Refrigerant and performance

01 to 07

Is the system charged correctly and doing the work it should?

  1. 01Check refrigerant levels
  2. 02Check superheat
  3. 03Check subcooling
  4. 04Check compression ratios
  5. 05Check temperature drop
  6. 06Calculate wattage usage
  7. 07Perform pump down test

Electrical

08 to 14

Are the parts that start and protect the system safe and sound?

  1. 08Inspect high voltage circuits
  2. 09Inspect low voltage circuits
  3. 10Measure amperage
  4. 11Inspect and test capacitor
  5. 12Check contactor for pitting or burnt spots
  6. 13Inspect disconnect and fuses
  7. 14Test safety controls

Airflow and drainage

15 to 23

Is air moving freely and is water draining where it should?

  1. 15Clean condenser coil
  2. 16Inspect condenser fan motor
  3. 17Inspect evaporator coil for proper drainage
  4. 18Inspect drain line
  5. 19Inspect condensate pump (where applicable)
  6. 20Inspect blower, lubricate if necessary
  7. 21Inspect belt on blower (where applicable)
  8. 22Inspect for proper airflow on outdoor unit
  9. 23Check that the outdoor unit is level

Controls and parts

24 to 29

Do the controls read true and the valves and fittings hold?

  1. 24Calibrate and adjust thermostat
  2. 25Inspect expansion valve
  3. 26Inspect reversing valve (where applicable)
  4. 27Adjust service valve caps and connections
  5. 28Shut down humidifier
  6. 29Change standard air filterFilter or pad beyond standard: extra cost

Starting at $75 per visit. A standard air filter change is included; a filter or pad beyond standard costs extra. The office confirms your price when it sets up the visit.

Where is Black's Heat & Air located?

Black's Heat & Air LLC is a family-owned heating and cooling company at 274 Smartt Station Road in Morrison, Tennessee. It serves McMinnville and the surrounding area.

How long has Black's Heat & Air been in business?

Black's Heat & Air has been family owned and operated since 1985.

Is Black's Heat & Air licensed and insured?

Yes. Black's Heat & Air is licensed, insured and a member of the NFIB.

What are Black's Heat & Air's hours?

The office is open Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 2 PM. The phone line, 931-668-0785, is answered 24/7, and the company handles emergency heating and cooling repairs.

What HVAC services does Black's Heat & Air offer?

Installation and replacement after a thorough exam of the existing unit, repair and maintenance, and indoor air quality work: duct cleaning, filter replacements, damper adjustments and air purifier installation.

How much is an HVAC inspection?

Black's Heat & Air's 29-point inspection starts at $75. It covers refrigerant levels, superheat and subcooling, coils, motors, drain lines, electrical circuits, capacitor and contactor, safety controls, thermostat calibration and a standard filter change. Filters or pads beyond standard cost extra.

Does Black's Heat & Air work on commercial buildings?

Yes. Black's Heat & Air serves residential and commercial clients and offers a full consultation to help choose a system for a home or business.

Does Black's Heat & Air offer free quotes, financing and a warranty?

Yes. Quotes are free by phone at 931-668-0785 or through the website form, financing is available through an online application, and a warranty is included with all services.
