Services

Start with the problem you have.

Six kinds of call cover most of what we do in homes. Each one is handled by a licensed plumber with the right equipment for finding the cause, not just the symptom.

Not sure which one it is? Describe it in a request
01

Leaks and slab leaks

Advanced electronic leak detection finds the leak before anything is opened up. The process is non-invasive, so there is little to no damage to your floors and walls.

  • Slab leaks under the foundation
  • Natural gas line leaks
  • AC and other pressurized systems
  • Cause-and-origin reports for insurance
Request leak detection
02

Drains and sewers

Drain cleaning and rooter service, with a video camera to see inside the line and track down the problem. When a line is damaged, we repair or replace it.

  • Video camera inspection and tracking
  • Water flushing and snaking
  • Sewer cleaning, repair and replacement
  • Pipe repair and replacement
Request drain or sewer service
03

Water heaters and tankless

We repair and replace all water heater makes and models, and install and replace tankless water heaters. Have yours serviced at least once a year.

Signs it needs a look: no hot water or inconsistent heat, a leaking tank, rattling noises, sediment buildup, water that smells.

Request water heater service
04

Gas lines and gas tests

Gas line repair and installation, and code-approved gas tests when a line is new, changed or in question.

  • Gas line repair
  • New gas line installation
  • Code-approved gas tests
  • Gas leak detection
Request gas line service
05

Water lines

Repair of broken or damaged water lines, from the line into the house to the pipes inside it.

Request water line repair
06

Backflow testing

Louisiana law requires every property with a backflow prevention device to have it tested and inspected once a year by a properly certified plumber. Terry holds the Water Supply Protection Specialist (WSPS) endorsement.

  • Annual testing and certification
  • Results filed through the AquaResource portal
  • Repair of failed assemblies
  • New backflow device installation
Request a backflow test

Questions

Common questions

Something not covered here? Call (225) 756-8480.

Who owns American Plumbing in Baton Rouge?

American Plumbing is owned and operated by Terry Smith, a Louisiana master plumber who opened the company in 1989 to serve the East Baton Rouge and Ascension areas. Its legal name is American Plumbing Mechanical & Maintenance Company, LLC.

Is American Plumbing licensed and insured?

Yes. American Plumbing holds Louisiana Master Plumber License #556, is bonded and insured, and holds the Water Supply Protection Specialist (WSPS) endorsement for backflow work. Every truck carries a licensed journeyman plumber.

Where is American Plumbing located and how do I reach it?

The office is at 6342 Quinn Drive, Baton Rouge, LA 70817. Call (225) 756-8480 in Baton Rouge or (225) 644-7748 in Gonzales, or email tsmith@americanplumbingla.com.

What are American Plumbing's hours?

The office is open Monday through Friday, 5:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and closed Saturday and Sunday.

Which areas does American Plumbing serve?

American Plumbing serves Baton Rouge, Gonzales, Central, Denham Springs, Prairieville, Port Allen and Brusly, Zachary, Walker, Plaquemine and Addis, and Donaldsonville.

Does American Plumbing work on commercial and industrial properties?

Yes. Besides homes, American Plumbing serves businesses from small offices to large warehouses and industrial facilities, with pipefitting, pump replacement, lift station installation and repair, floor drains, gas lines, water heaters and video pipe inspection.

Does my backflow preventer need to be tested every year?

Yes. Louisiana law requires every property with a backflow prevention device to have it tested and inspected once a year by a properly certified plumber. American Plumbing tests, certifies, repairs and installs backflow assemblies and files results through the AquaResource portal.

What plumbing services does American Plumbing offer at home?

Water heater repair and replacement for all makes and models, tankless installation, electronic leak detection including slab leaks, drain cleaning and rooter service, video pipe inspection, sewer repair and replacement, gas line repair and code-approved gas tests, waterline repair and backflow testing.

Does American Plumbing offer any discounts?

Discounts may be available for military families, first responder families and seniors over 65.
