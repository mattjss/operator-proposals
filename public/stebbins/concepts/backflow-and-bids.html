      03 Backflow testing explained

      One-way water, checked and signed off.

      A backflow preventer keeps water from running backward out of your building's plumbing and into the public supply. Stebbins tests, rebuilds and replaces them throughout the Greater Dayton area.

      1. Request
        Tell us about the device

        Use the request builder, or text a photo of your test notice to (937) 314-4501.

      2. 1 day
        We call to schedule

        Within one business day, for a weekday between 9 AM and 3 PM.

      3. 10–15 min
        Water off, briefly

        The water is shut off for about 10 to 15 minutes while the device is tested.

      4. If it fails
        Rebuild or replace

        We rebuild or replace backflow preventers, so one crew can see it through.

      Who does the testing: Paul “Willie” is the team's Backflow Specialist.

      Where is Stebbins Plumbing & Heating located?

      Stebbins Plumbing & Heating is a family-owned plumbing company at 621 East Dixie Drive in West Carrollton, Ohio 45449, serving the Greater Dayton area including West Carrollton and Miamisburg.

      How long has Stebbins Plumbing been in business?

      Stebbins Plumbing was founded in 1926 by Cecil Stebbins as a small plumbing shop and celebrated its 100th anniversary in 2026.

      Who owns Stebbins Plumbing & Heating?

      Bruce Stebbins is President and Owner. He acquired the business through working with his father, uncles and aunts, and the company remains family owned.

      How do I reach Stebbins Plumbing?

      Call 937-859-4534, text 937-314-4501 (texts only), or email stebbins621@gmail.com. Service hours are Monday through Friday.

      What plumbing services does Stebbins offer?

      Drain cleaning and maintenance, water heater service and installation, fixture sales and installation, new and replacement parts, backflow testing and repair, and commercial new construction plumbing, including fuel stations.

      What water heaters does Stebbins install?

      Stebbins offers American-made water heaters from A. O. Smith and Bradford White, plus tankless lines.

      Does Stebbins test backflow preventers?

      Yes. Stebbins tests, rebuilds and replaces backflow preventers throughout the Greater Dayton area. Testing is scheduled Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 3 PM, and the water is shut off for about 10 to 15 minutes.

      How do I get an estimate from Stebbins?

      Submit the Estimate/Bid Request form on stebbinsplumbing.com with your project details and files. Someone will be in touch within two business days.

      Is Stebbins a member of a trade association?

      Yes. Stebbins Plumbing & Heating is a longstanding member of the Dayton PHCC (Plumbing-Heating-Cooling Contractors) organization.
