Working with your builder

One shop from the estimate to the years after.

B & B's new construction department works the same four steps on every house, so you and your builder always know who's next on site.

  1. Estimate

    Bring your plans and your systems sheet. Estimates are free, and the showroom lets you see fixtures and fireplaces before you choose.

    Free · no obligation

  2. Rough-in

    Water and drain lines, gas piping, ductwork and venting go in while the walls are open, in step with your builder's schedule.

    Framing stage

  3. Finish

    Fixtures, water heater, furnace or heat pump, softener and fireplace are set, connected and started so the house is ready to live in.

    Before move-in

  4. Service after

    “We service anything we sell.” The same local shop is a phone call away when the house needs a tune-up or a repair.

    For the life of the house

    Questions

    Good to know before you build.

    Don't see yours? Call the shop at (712) 722-2750.

    Where is B & B Plumbing & Heating located?

    B & B Plumbing & Heating, Inc. is a family-owned plumbing, heating and cooling company at 3756 US Highway 75 Avenue on the north end of Sioux Center, Iowa. It serves homes within about 20 to 30 miles of Sioux Center.

    How long has B & B Plumbing & Heating been in business?

    B & B was founded in Sioux Center on March 2, 1976, by Keith Bonnema and Glen Brower, and is now owned by Mark Bonnema and his sons.

    What are B & B's office hours?

    The office is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM. Call (712) 722-2750 or email billie@bbplumb.com for a quote or appointment.

    Is B & B licensed?

    Yes. B & B is licensed in plumbing, heating, air conditioning and hydronic systems, with more than nine licensed technicians and over 120 years of combined experience.

    What plumbing services does B & B offer?

    Faucets, toilets, tub and shower units, sinks, whirlpools and garbage disposals; tank and tankless water heaters; reverse osmosis systems; and water softeners, for homes and commercial customers. B & B services anything it sells.

    What heating and cooling services does B & B offer?

    Furnace, heat pump and air conditioner installation and repair with Amana and Rheem equipment, Bosch geothermal, Mitsubishi mini-splits, Broan air exchangers, UV air purification and whole-home humidification. B & B is a licensed Mitsubishi Diamond Dealer.

    Does B & B install fireplaces?

    Yes. B & B sells, installs and services mainly Kozy Heat gas fireplaces and decorative stoves, plus electric fireplaces for homes where gas venting is difficult.

    Can B & B help with energy rebates?

    Yes. B & B helps customers with energy-efficiency rebate paperwork for MidAmerican Energy, Northwest REC and City of Sioux Center programs.

    How can I pay my B & B bill?

    Pay online at bbplumb.com/pay-your-bill with a debit or credit card. B & B accepts Mastercard, Visa and Discover, plus Sioux Center Chamber Dollars.
