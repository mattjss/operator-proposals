Sewer services

Sewer work, in the order the job happens.

Slow drains, gurgling and water in the basement can all start in the sewer line. The right fix depends on what is in the pipe, so every job starts with a look inside.

01Inspect

Sewer camera inspections and CCTV drain surveys

We run CCTV drain surveys to see what is going on inside the pipe instead of guessing. Advanced camera diagnostics show where a clog, root intrusion, crack or backup starts, so the fix you are quoted matches what is in the line.

  • Sewer camera inspectionFree estimate
  • CCTV drain surveyFree estimate
Request an inspection
02Clear

Sewer rodding and hydro jetting

Sewer rodding is mechanical cleaning that clears blockages and restores flow. We also hydro jet main sewer lines and kitchen-sink lines.

We recommend cleaning the sewer line every 18 to 22 months.

  • Sewer roddingMain line
  • Sewer hydro jettingMain line
  • Kitchen-sink hydro jettingKitchen drain
Book a cleaning
03Repair

Sewer repairs and trenchless lining

We handle clogs, cracks, roots and backups. Where the pipe can be saved, sewer lining is a no-dig trenchless repair that restores the pipe from the inside.

  • Sewer line repairs$3,000–$8,000
  • Trenchless sewer lining$4,000–$12,000
See how pricing works
04Replace

Excavation, full replacement and new sewer installation

When a line has to come out, we do the complete property assessment and handle the permits, open the line by excavation or trenchless access, set new PVC pipe, then test and inspect the work for full compliance.

  • Full sewer replacement$6,000–$15,000
  • Sewer installationFree estimate
  • PermitsHandled by us

What happens next

From inspection to a tested line.

The camera tells us what the job is. Here is how a sewer repair or replacement runs from there.

  1. Step 1

    Assessment and permits

    A complete property assessment, the price before we start, and the permits handled by us.

  2. Step 2

    Excavation or trenchless access

    We open the line the way the job needs: a trench, or no-dig trenchless access.

  3. Step 3

    Repair or replace

    Roots, cracks and failed sections are cleared, lined or replaced with new pipe.

  4. Step 4

    Testing and inspection

    The finished line is tested and inspected for full compliance.
