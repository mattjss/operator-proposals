Services

Sewer work and plumbing for your home

Most of our bigger jobs fall into two groups: the sewer line under your yard, and the equipment and fixtures inside your house. Every one starts with a free estimate.

Sewer and drains

Inspection, cleaning, repair and replacement of the line that carries everything out of your home, with permits handled and testing for compliance.

Camera inspections

We run CCTV drain surveys to see what is actually going on inside the pipe instead of guessing, so the repair fits the problem.

Free estimate

Rodding and hydro jetting

Mechanical rodding clears blockages and restores flow. Hydro jetting cleans sewer lines and kitchen sink drains.

Our guidance: have the sewer cleaned every 18 to 22 months.

Free estimate

Sewer repair

We handle clogs, cracks, roots and backups, including the tree roots that work their way into older lines.

Free estimate

Trenchless lining

No-dig trenchless sewer repair that restores the pipe from the inside, so your lawn and driveway stay where they are.

Free estimate

Sewer replacement and installation

Excavation and new sewer sections or full lines, with the property assessed, permits handled and the finished line tested. A sewer line can last 40 to 100 years.

Free estimate

Plumbing in your home

Repairs and replacements for the equipment and fixtures you use every day. We carry parts for water pumps and can usually install the same day you call.

Water heaters

We install and repair tank and tankless units. Most water heaters last 8 to 12 years; past 10 years, or with frequent repairs, replacement is often more cost-effective.

Rumbling or popping usually means sediment. Annual maintenance flushes it out and checks the valves and safety controls.

Free estimate

Sump, ejector and water pumps

Sump pump installation and replacement keeps your basement dry. Ejector pumps stop sewage from backing up into your lower level. Water pump supply and installation, usually same day.

Free estimate

Softeners, filtration and testing

Water softener installation and repair for cleaner, softer water that protects your plumbing and appliances, plus water filtration and real lab-based water analysis.

Free estimate

Leaks and water lines

Pipe leak detection and repair to stop water damage, and new water line installation for a clean, reliable supply.

Free estimate

Bathrooms and toilets

Sink and shower installations, faucet and valve repairs, and shower valve repair and replacement. We repair toilet leaks and clogs, replace faulty parts and install new high-efficiency toilets.

Free estimate

Spigots

Spigot replacement to stop leaks and restore proper water flow, indoors or outdoors.

To confirm: gas line installation (mentioned in the blog only)
