Common questions.

Who is EWS Plumbing & Heating?

EWS Plumbing & Heating, Inc. is a family-owned and operated plumbing, heating and air conditioning company at 339 Main St in Monson, Massachusetts, established in 1976 by Gary Stahelski.

Is EWS licensed and insured?

Yes. EWS is fully licensed and insured in Massachusetts and Connecticut. Founder Gary Stahelski holds master plumber and gasfitter licenses in Massachusetts and Connecticut and is a master sheet metal mechanic. Vice President Brant Stahelski holds master plumber and gasfitter licenses in Massachusetts, Connecticut and Vermont and is a master sheet metal mechanic in Massachusetts.

What area does EWS serve?

EWS serves customers from Vermont through the entire state of Massachusetts to the southern borders of Connecticut, from its office in Monson, Massachusetts.

Does EWS work on homes and commercial buildings?

Yes. EWS does residential and commercial plumbing, heating and air conditioning for homeowners, businesses, municipalities and institutions, including office buildings and retail establishments.

What residential services does EWS offer?

Plumbing installation and repair for new builds and remodels, gas and oil heating installation and repair, tank and tankless water heaters, ductless mini-split heat pumps, natural gas and propane piping, and sheet metal work including ductwork, exhaust systems and ERV/HRV systems.

Is EWS a Mass Save contractor?

Yes. EWS is a Mass Save participating contractor. Its mini-split heat pumps qualify for Mass Save and rebate programs, and EWS helps customers with the application paperwork.

Does EWS service commercial rooftop units?

Yes. EWS installs new and upgraded rooftop units for commercial properties, assesses and upgrades their energy efficiency, and maintains them with inspections, cleaning, lubrication and adjustments.

What commercial HVAC systems does EWS work on?

EWS installs and repairs rooftop units, boilers and hydronic systems, mini splits and VRF systems, and designs, installs and maintains commercial plumbing systems.

How do I reach EWS?

Call EWS Plumbing & Heating at 413-267-8983 or visit the office at 339 Main St, Monson, MA 01057.
