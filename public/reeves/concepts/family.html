Our story

From a farm and a GE welding job to a master plumber's license.

Reeves started with one man from Erie who learned the trade the long way.

Richard “Rick” Reeves worked on a farm until he was 18. He then spent ten years as a welder at GE. After that he spent fourteen years learning plumbing under a local plumber before he earned his master plumber's license and started his own company.

The company is still family owned and operated. From the home office on West 21st Street in Erie it has grown to offices in Eastlake and Brook Park, Ohio, and a line for Youngstown, Warren and Akron. It trains its own technicians, and it stays close to Erie's schools, teams and charities.

Drains and sewer lining

Residential drain cleaning 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with commercial drain cleaning equipment. A camera lets us see and record the inside of your drain line and locate it underground.

When the sewer line itself is failing, Reeves can line it with cured-in-place pipe (CIPP). It is a trenchless repair, so there is far less digging through your lawn, landscaping and concrete.

  • Camera viewing
  • Underground locating
  • Trenchless CIPP lining

Questions

Before you call.

Still not sure? Call any office. Someone answers, any hour.

Where is Reeves Plumbing & Heating located?

Reeves Plumbing & Heating is a family-owned plumbing, heating and cooling company based at 1850 West 21st in Erie, Pennsylvania. It also has Ohio locations at 1630 East 361st in Eastlake and 5297 W 137th St Unit 8 in Brook Park, and serves Youngstown, Warren and Akron.

How long has Reeves Plumbing & Heating been in business?

Reeves Plumbing & Heating has served Northwest Pennsylvania since 1966. The company was founded by Richard Reeves of Erie, a former GE welder who trained for fourteen years under a local plumber before earning his master plumber's license.

Does Reeves offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes. Reeves answers its phone 24/7, 365 days a year. For emergency or after-hours service, call the office: Erie (814) 459-0004, Eastlake (440) 701-4543, Brook Park (216) 258-0424, or Youngstown, Warren and Akron (330) 449-4792.

What services does Reeves Plumbing & Heating provide?

Reeves provides residential and commercial plumbing, drain cleaning, camera inspection and underground locating, furnace, air conditioner and boiler repair and replacement, duct work, yearly HVAC maintenance, tank and tankless water heaters, and trenchless sewer lining.

Is Reeves Plumbing & Heating licensed?

Yes. Reeves holds Erie Master licenses #584 and #538, Erie Master Appliance #AP350, PA Contractor #00197, Ohio license #47749, and Mahoning County HVAC Residential & Commercial #48135.

What warranty does Reeves give on a new furnace or air conditioner?

Reeves warranties parts and labor for 10 years from the date of installation on its furnace and air conditioner installs, provided the system is serviced at least once every 365 days. The warranty is not transferable to new owners.

Can I fix a sewer line without digging up my yard?

Reeves offers trenchless sewer lining using cured-in-place pipe. The liner is installed through small entry points, which helps minimize damage to landscaping and concrete.

Does Reeves offer financing?

Yes. In Erie, customers can borrow up to $15,000 through a personal loan from Erie Federal Credit Union, and Reeves also offers a 12-month, no-interest layaway program with a 25% deposit.

Which water heater brands does Reeves install?

Reeves sizes, installs, repairs and replaces natural gas, propane and electric water heaters, including State, A.O. Smith and Bradford White, and installs Navien tankless water heaters and boilers.
