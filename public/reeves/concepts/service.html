Services

Plumbing, drains and sewer

Leaks, slow or backed-up drains, water in the basement, a sewer line that keeps failing.

  • Plumbing repair

    Leaky faucets, broken drains and pipe repairs by certified technicians.

  • Residential drain cleaning

    Kitchen, bath and main drains cleared with commercial drain cleaning equipment. Available 24 hours, 7 days a week by phone.

  • Camera viewing

    A camera sees and records the inside of your drainage system, so the blockage or break is found, not guessed at.

  • Underground locating

    Pinpoints where a buried line runs and where the problem sits before any repair is planned.

  • Preventive maintenance

    Plumbing maintenance options that catch small problems before they turn into a flooded basement.

Trenchless

Sewer lining with cured-in-place pipe (CIPP)

Reeves repairs failing sewer lines with trenchless technology. A liner is cured inside the old pipe, so there's no trench across the yard and far less damage to landscaping, concrete and other large obstructions.

Heating and cooling

No heat, no cooling, a boiler that won't fire, or a system that's due for its yearly check-up.

  • Furnace repair or replacement

    Diagnosis and repair, or a new furnace from Reeves's own HVAC brand.

  • Air conditioner repair or replacement

    Central air repaired, or replaced with a Reeves-brand system.

  • Boiler repair or replacement

    Hot water and steam boilers repaired or replaced, including Navien boilers.

  • Duct work

    Duct repair or replacement for a home or business comfort system.

  • Yearly maintenance check-ups

    One visit every 365 days keeps a Reeves furnace or AC warranty active. Pennsylvania customers can claim Penelec's $50 AC tune-up rebate.

10 years parts and labor on Reeves furnace and AC installs

Covers Reeves-brand equipment when the system is serviced at least once every 365 days. Not transferable to a new owner. Without yearly service it reverts to the standard 1-year warranty.

Water heaters

No hot water, a leaking tank, or an old unit you'd rather replace before it fails.

  • Tank water heaters

    Natural gas, propane or electric. Reeves sizes, installs, repairs and replaces State, A.O. Smith and Bradford White units. A tank lasts about five to ten years.

  • Tankless water heaters

    Upgrade to a Navien tankless unit for higher efficiency and gas savings. Tankless can last up to twenty years, and Navien tankless heaters and boilers qualify for a federal 30% tax credit up to $600.

  • Emergency replacement

    24-hour emergency water heater replacement. Call the office nearest you; the line is answered 24/7.

FAQ

Questions before you call.

Where is Reeves Plumbing & Heating located?

Reeves Plumbing & Heating is a family-owned plumbing, heating and cooling company based at 1850 West 21st in Erie, Pennsylvania. It also has Ohio locations at 1630 East 361st in Eastlake and 5297 W 137th St Unit 8 in Brook Park, and serves Youngstown, Warren and Akron.

How long has Reeves Plumbing & Heating been in business?

Reeves Plumbing & Heating has served Northwest Pennsylvania since 1966. The company was founded by Richard Reeves of Erie, a former GE welder who trained for fourteen years under a local plumber before earning his master plumber's license.

Does Reeves offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes. Reeves answers its phone 24/7, 365 days a year. For emergency or after-hours service, call the office: Erie (814) 459-0004, Eastlake (440) 701-4543, Brook Park (216) 258-0424, or Youngstown, Warren and Akron (330) 449-4792.

What services does Reeves Plumbing & Heating provide?

Reeves provides residential and commercial plumbing, drain cleaning, camera inspection and underground locating, furnace, air conditioner and boiler repair and replacement, duct work, yearly HVAC maintenance, tank and tankless water heaters, and trenchless sewer lining.

Is Reeves Plumbing & Heating licensed?

Yes. Reeves holds Erie Master licenses #584 and #538, Erie Master Appliance #AP350, PA Contractor #00197, Ohio license #47749, and Mahoning County HVAC Residential & Commercial #48135.

What warranty does Reeves give on a new furnace or air conditioner?

Reeves warranties parts and labor for 10 years from the date of installation on its furnace and air conditioner installs, provided the system is serviced at least once every 365 days. The warranty is not transferable to new owners.

Can I fix a sewer line without digging up my yard?

Reeves offers trenchless sewer lining using cured-in-place pipe. The liner is installed through small entry points, which helps minimize damage to landscaping and concrete.

Does Reeves offer financing?

Yes. In Erie, customers can borrow up to $15,000 through a personal loan from Erie Federal Credit Union, and Reeves also offers a 12-month, no-interest layaway program with a 25% deposit.

Which water heater brands does Reeves install?

Reeves sizes, installs, repairs and replaces natural gas, propane and electric water heaters, including State, A.O. Smith and Bradford White, and installs Navien tankless water heaters and boilers.
