Navien tankless

Tankless water heater or combi boiler? Here is the difference.

Both are wall-mounted Navien units, and both replace a bulky tank. Which one fits depends on how your home is heated today.

Navien

Certified Navien partner and service specialistFind Izzy's in the official Navien contractor directory. To confirm: Navien partner tier

Navien tankless water heater

In one line
Heats water only when you need it.
What it does
Heats water as it flows to the tap, so there is no tank of stored hot water to run out or keep warm.
It replaces
A tank water heater.
Suits homes that
Run short of hot water, or want back the floor space a tank takes up.

Navien combi boiler

In one line
Home heating and hot water from one unit.
What it does
Runs your radiators or baseboard heat and also heats water for taps and showers on demand.
It replaces
A boiler and its separate water heater.
Suits homes that
Already heat with a boiler and want one unit doing the job of two.
