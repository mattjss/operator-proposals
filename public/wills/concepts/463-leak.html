Hidden leak? Try the meter test

Is water leaving when nothing's on?

A high water bill, a damp spot, a hiss in the wall. Before you call, your water meter can tell you a lot. Three steps, about as long as a coffee break.

Step 1: Turn off every fixture

Inside and out. Check each one off as you go; the meter on the left slows down with you.

5 left to turn off

Step 2: Find the low-flow indicator

On most meters it's a small triangle, star or gear on the face, separate from the big sweep hand. It turns with even a slow drip, so it shows leaks the hand can't.

The meter is usually where the water line comes into the house or in a pit near the street. Can't find it, or the lid is frozen shut? Call us instead.

Can't find it: call

Step 3: Wait, then recheck

Note where the indicator points and the last digit on the counter. Leave every fixture off for a while, then look again. Did anything move?

Spinning? You may have a hidden leak.

With everything off, the meter shouldn't move. Something is still using water: a running toilet, a slab or line leak, a fixture you missed. Call 307-463-LEAK and Will's can confirm it with leak detection.

Still? Good sign.

No movement with everything off usually means no hidden leak right now. Still seeing a wet spot, a high bill or a sound in the wall? Call and we'll take a look.
