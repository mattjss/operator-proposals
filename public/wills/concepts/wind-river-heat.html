02 / DELIVERY

In-floor heating installations

Warm water running through tubing under the floor heats the room from the ground up: no vents, no blowing air, and warm feet on a January morning.

  • Concrete slabTubing set in the slab of a main floor or basement.
  • Upstairs, wood-frame floorsTubing run under the subfloor between the joists.
  • Garages and shopsA heated slab where trucks come in snowy and work goes on all winter.

03 / HOT WATER

Water heater services

Installation, repair and maintenance, so there's hot water for every shower. If you already have a boiler, ask whether an indirect tank off the boiler makes sense for your house.

  • InstallationReplacing a tired tank or adding hot water to a new space.
  • RepairNo hot water, not enough, or a tank that's leaking.
  • MaintenanceKeep it running longer, especially on hard water.

04 / Better water

Water softening & filtration

Hard water leaves scale in pipes, water heaters and boilers. Will's installs softening and filtration systems with Cascadian's technology for cleaner, safer water that's easier on everything it runs through.

Ask about water treatment
  • Cleaner, safer waterExpert installation of Cascadian softening and filtration.
  • Longer plumbing lifeLess buildup inside pipes and fixtures.
  • Protects appliancesLess scale on the water heater, the boiler and everything else that uses your water.
