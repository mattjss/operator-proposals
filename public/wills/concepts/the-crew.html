Will's story

Started plumbing right out of high school.

Will Goehring went to work as a plumber right out of high school. Today he's a Master Plumber with more than 30 years of hands-on experience, and Will's Plumbing & Heating has served Riverton since 2004.

It's a small shop. Will leads a crew of five from 2621 Airport Drive, working on homes and businesses within 50 miles of town: everyday plumbing repairs and drains, water heaters, boilers and in-floor heat for long Wyoming winters, and water softening and filtration.

Our promise, in writing

One year on our workmanship. Here's how it works.

Will's General Labor & Workmanship Warranty, in four plain steps. Walk through it before you hire us.

  1. 1

    One year on the work

    Our workmanship is warranted to be free from defects for 1 year from the date the job is completed.

    Job finished? Your year starts that day.

  2. 2

    Tell us in writing within 15 days

    Send your claim in writing within 15 days, with proof of payment and photos of the problem.

    Email works: office@willsplumbingheating.com.

  3. 3

    We fix it within 60 days

    Once your claim is in, the crew has up to 60 days to make the repair.

    Questions about a claim? Call the office at 307-463-LEAK.

  4. 4

    It stays with you, not the house

    The warranty doesn't transfer. If the property is sold, it ends.

    The office can walk you through the full written terms.

Questions

Asked at the door

Don't see yours? Call 307-463-LEAK and ask the office.

Where is Will's Plumbing & Heating located?

Will's Plumbing & Heating, Inc. is at 2621 Airport Dr in Riverton, Wyoming. It serves customers within a 50-mile radius of Riverton.

Who owns Will's Plumbing & Heating?

Will's Plumbing & Heating was founded by Will Goehring, a Master Plumber with more than 30 years of hands-on experience who started plumbing right out of high school.

How long has Will's Plumbing & Heating been in business?

Will's Plumbing & Heating has provided plumbing and heating services in Riverton since 2004.

What are the office hours?

The office is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Call 307-463-LEAK (5325) or email office@willsplumbingheating.com; messages are answered within 24 business hours.

Does Will's offer emergency service?

Yes. Emergency service is available after hours by appointment only. Call 307-463-5325.

What services does Will's Plumbing & Heating offer?

Plumbing repairs, water heater installation, repair and maintenance, leak detection, drain cleaning and hydro jetting, drain replacement, boiler installation and maintenance, in-floor heating installations, and water softening and filtration systems using Cascadian technology.

Does Will's work on homes and businesses?

Yes. Will's Plumbing & Heating serves both residential and commercial clients in Riverton and the surrounding area.

Is the Will's team certified?

Yes. The technician team is Level 3 Navien certified for high-efficiency systems, and the team includes a Master Plumber and a Journeyman Plumber.

Is the work warranted?

Yes. Will's warrants its workmanship to be free from defects for one year from the date of completion, under its General Labor & Workmanship Warranty.
