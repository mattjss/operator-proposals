Services

Asphalt shingle roofs, and the work around them.

Most of what we do is on the roof itself: reroofs, repairs and storm damage. We also install VELUX skylights and seamless gutters, and roof new homes, additions and remodels for Clark County builders.

01

Roofs

Tear-offs and reroofing

When a roof is past repair or at the end of its useful life, we tear it off and replace it. We can complete most reroofing projects in one day.

Shingles: IKO, Malarkey, PABCO and CertainTeed, in a range of asphalt shingle colors.

To confirm: shingle color samples Ask for a reroof estimate

Roof repairs and leaks

Roof leak repair and prevention, handled promptly and efficiently. Putting off maintenance can cost more later, so it pays to look at a leak early.

Ask about a repair

Storm damage and debris hauling

A storm can cause severe damage to your roof. We repair or replace storm-damaged roofs, some the same day, and haul away and dispose of the storm debris.

To confirm: insurance claim help Report storm damage

Roof condition checks

Curling or buckling shingles, granular loss and leaks are the signs to watch. When shingles curl they can cause serious structural damage. Not sure whether to repair or replace? Call for an inspection.

Ask for an inspection
02

Around the roof

VELUX skylights

New skylight installs, repairs, flashing and replacements with VELUX. A skylight brings natural light into any room and may lower energy bills, and we stand behind our work.

Ask about a skylight

Seamless gutters

Seamless gutter installation, removal of old or damaged systems, and custom-fit replacements that match your home’s exterior. Good gutters protect against water damage and foundation issues. No job too small.

Ask about gutters
03

Building

For homeowners and builders planning a new home, an addition or a remodel.

New construction, additions and remodels

Asphalt shingle roofs for new homes, additions and remodels. Expect quality workmanship and a quick turnaround time. We are members of the Building Industry Association of Clark County.

Questions

Common questions before an estimate.

Where is Ed's Roofing located?

Ed's Roofing, LLC is a local, family-owned and operated roofing company at 15805 NE Caples Rd. in Brush Prairie, Washington. Its mailing address is PO Box 249, Brush Prairie, WA 98606.

How long has Ed's Roofing been in business?

Ed's Roofing has served Clark County, Washington since 1977, and its trained staff has over 100 years of combined experience in the roofing industry.

What areas does Ed's Roofing serve?

Ed's Roofing serves Clark County, Washington, including Vancouver, Camas, Washougal, Ridgefield, La Center, Woodland, Battle Ground, Yacolt and Amboy.

What roofing services does Ed's Roofing offer?

Roof tear-offs and reroofing, roof repairs and leak repair, storm damage repair and debris hauling, new construction, additions and remodeling roofs, VELUX skylight installation and repair, roof condition inspections, and seamless gutter installation and replacement.

Does Ed's Roofing give free estimates?

Yes. Estimates are free. Call 360-687-2963, email edsroof@gmail.com, or use the instant online roofing estimate on the Ed's Roofing website.

How long does a reroof take?

Ed's Roofing says it can complete most reroofing projects in one day.

Which shingle brands does Ed's Roofing install?

Ed's Roofing installs asphalt shingles from IKO, Malarkey, PABCO and CertainTeed, in a range of colors. For skylights it installs VELUX.

What are Ed's Roofing's office hours?

The office is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 am to 4:00 pm, and closed Saturday and Sunday. The main phone number is 360-687-2963.

Has Ed's Roofing won any awards?

Yes. Ed's Roofing has been named a Best of Clark County roofer in 2016, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024 and 2025. It also describes itself as a BBB-accredited business and a member of the Building Industry Association of Clark County.
