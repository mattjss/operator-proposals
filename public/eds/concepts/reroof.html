Tear-offs and reroofing

Signs your roof is ready to be replaced

When a roof is past repair or past its useful life, a full tear-off and reroof is the fix. Putting off maintenance can cost more later.

  1. 01

    Curling or buckling shingles

    Edges lift, corners cup or rows ripple. In Ed's words: When shingles curl they can cause serious structural damage.

    Not sure? Ask for an inspection
  2. 02

    Granule loss

    Bare, shiny patches on the shingles and grit collecting in the gutters are signs the surface is wearing away.

    Not sure? Ask for an inspection
  3. 03

    Leaks

    A stain on the ceiling or water in the attic. Sometimes a repair is enough, sometimes the whole roof is due. The crew looks first and tells you which.

    Not sure? Ask for an inspection
