        FAQ

        Where is Denny's Roofing located?

        Denny's Roofing, LLC is a locally owned roofing contractor with its main office at 2330 Main St Unit D in Longmont, Colorado. It serves the greater Colorado Front Range and the Western Slope, from Fort Collins and Greeley to Denver, Highlands Ranch and Grand Junction. Call 970-275-4012.

        Who owns Denny's Roofing?

        Denny's Roofing was founded by Denny Phillips, a Colorado native who worked for large and small Colorado roofing companies for more than ten years before starting his own. As owner, he says he inspects every job himself.

        Does Denny's Roofing charge for inspections or estimates?

        No. Denny's Roofing gives free roof inspections and free estimates to all potential clients, with no obligation and no pressure.

        Can Denny's Roofing help with my hail damage insurance claim?

        Yes. Denny's Roofing inspects the roof, helps open the claim if it finds damage, meets your insurance adjuster on site, requests supplements for code-required items, pulls the building permit, and reviews the final scope with you line by line before you pay the final invoice.

        Can a roofing contractor pay or waive my deductible in Colorado?

        No. Under Colorado law a roofing contractor cannot pay, waive, rebate or promise to pay a homeowner's insurance deductible. You pay your deductible to the contractor doing the work.

        How long does a roof replacement take?

        For a residential roof, removal and replacement usually takes one to two days, depending on the size of the roof. Denny's Roofing performs nail sweeps during clean-up and arranges the final city or county inspection.

        Does Denny's Roofing offer impact-resistant shingles?

        Yes. Denny's Roofing offers a free upgrade to Class IV impact-resistant shingles in most situations, and can provide a certification to send to your insurance carrier, which may offer a premium discount.

        Does Denny's Roofing do commercial roofing?

        Yes. Denny's Roofing serves Colorado business owners and property managers and specializes in flat and low-slope commercial roofing, including TPO, EPDM, modified bitumen, PVC, rubber and coated roofs, as well as standing seam metal.

        Does Denny's Roofing offer any discounts?

        Yes. With a full roof replacement, Denny's Roofing offers a $250 military discount, a $250 senior discount for customers 65 and older, and a $250 referral fee for referring a friend.
