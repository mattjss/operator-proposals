In-floor radiant heat

Pick a room. Turn on the floor.

With in-floor heat, warm water from a boiler runs through tubing under the floor, so heat rises from below instead of blowing out of a vent. See how it looks in the room you're planning.

    Boilers

    The boiler is the heart of a warm floor.

    In-floor heat is only as good as the boiler behind it. Block installs new boilers, cleans the ones you have, and repairs them when they quit.

    Installation

    New boilers for new homes and renovations, and replacement of older units. Can be planned together with in-floor heat.

    Cleaning & maintenance

    Preventative maintenance on existing boilers, best booked before the first hard freeze rather than during it.

    Repair

    Repairs on existing units. If the heat goes out at night or on a weekend, the 24-hour emergency line is 507-451-9545.
