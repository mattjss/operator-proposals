Questions

Plain answers

Something else on your mind? Call the office at 507-451-9545.

Where is Block Plumbing & Heating located?

Block Plumbing & Heating is a locally owned and operated plumbing and heating company at 2424 Hoffman Drive in Owatonna, Minnesota.

How long has Block Plumbing & Heating been in business?

Block Plumbing & Heating has provided plumbing services in Owatonna since 1947.

Does Block Plumbing & Heating offer emergency service?

Yes. Block Plumbing & Heating offers 24-hour emergency service. Call 507-451-9545.

What are Block Plumbing & Heating's office hours?

The office is open Monday through Thursday 8:00 am to 5:00 pm and Friday 8:00 am to 4:30 pm, and closed Saturday and Sunday. Emergency service is available 24 hours.

Is Block Plumbing & Heating licensed?

Yes. Block Plumbing & Heating's plumbers are licensed and insured, and the company lists license numbers 063264PM and PC643816. Keith Martin is its Master Plumber.

What services does Block Plumbing & Heating provide?

Plumbing repairs, gas, electric and tankless water heaters, boiler repair and installation, sewer and drain cleaning, preventative maintenance, remodels, new construction, water softeners, in-floor heating, garbage disposals, faucets, sump pumps, backflow prevention, walk-in tubs and ADA certified toilets and showers.

Does Block Plumbing & Heating work on commercial buildings?

Yes. Block Plumbing & Heating serves both residential and commercial properties.

Which water heater and fixture brands does Block Plumbing & Heating install?

Block Plumbing & Heating stocks AO Smith standard and high-efficiency water heaters and services and installs brands including American Standard, AO Smith, Delta, Kohler, Moen and Navien. It can help with rebate paperwork.

How can I pay Block Plumbing & Heating?

Block Plumbing & Heating accepts Visa, MasterCard, Discover, debit, check and cash.
