Replace a system

How a replacement day works

Our installers are trained on current equipment, and our fleet of replacement trucks brings the shop to you. That is why most furnace and air conditioner replacements are finished in a single day.

  1. 01

    In-home consult

    A comfort consultant visits, looks at your current equipment and ductwork, and listens to what isn't working: rooms that run cold, noise, dry winter air.

  2. 02

    Sizing the system

    The new equipment is sized for your house. Then you choose between Trane and Bryant options at the efficiency that fits your budget.

  3. 03

    Install from a Shop on Wheels truck

    The crew arrives with the shop on board, so fittings and adjustments happen in your driveway instead of across town. Most installs are done the same day.

  4. 04

    Isolation pads and balancing dampers

    We set the furnace on isolation pads to cut vibration and add balancing dampers so air can be adjusted room by room.

  5. 05

    Walkthrough and warranty

    Before the crew leaves, you see the new system and thermostat running. Installed parts and labor are covered for the first year of operation.

Geothermal

Geothermal for new and existing homes

A geothermal heat pump moves heat between your home and the ground. There are no flues, no flame and no carbon monoxide, and one system heats and cools.

Colony specializes in geothermal for new homes and for existing homes replacing a furnace and air conditioner. Our geothermal work is led by Tim Stecklein, who has been with Colony since 1988.

Where is Colony Plumbing, Heating and Air Conditioning located?

Colony Plumbing, Heating and Air Conditioning is at 2224 16th Ave SW in Cedar Rapids, Iowa, where it also runs a fireplace showroom. It serves Cedar Rapids, Marion, Coralville, Iowa City, North Liberty, Mount Vernon, Anamosa and surrounding communities.

How long has Colony been in business?

Colony opened in 1973 as a small two-man shop in the Amana Colonies and settled in Cedar Rapids in 1981. Brian Miller has led the company as President since 2020.

Does Colony offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes. Colony's technicians serve residential, commercial and industrial customers 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, including heating, air conditioning, plumbing and geothermal repair. Call (319) 364-4328 (319-364-HEAT).

What are Colony's office and showroom hours?

The office and showroom are open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM. On Saturday the showroom is closed and open by appointment only, and it is closed on Sunday.

What services does Colony provide?

Colony provides heating and air conditioning service, repair and replacement, geothermal systems, residential new construction HVAC, commercial HVAC design-build and service, plumbing and bathroom remodels, water heaters, water conditioning, and fireplaces, stoves and inserts.

Is Colony a Trane dealer?

Yes. Colony installs Trane furnaces and air conditioners and promotes its Trane Comfort Specialist status. It also offers Bryant furnaces.

What warranty does Colony provide on installations?

Colony warranties all installed parts and labor for the first year of operation. Honeywell thermostats installed by Colony carry a 5-year parts and 1-year labor warranty.

Does Colony offer financing and maintenance plans?

Yes. Colony offers financing with approved credit through Wells Fargo, commercial financing through Navitas, and a prepaid annual HVAC Membership Program in three tiers: Better ($498), Best ($699) and Fantastic ($798) per system.

Does Colony handle commercial projects?

Yes. Colony's Commercial Division provides in-house engineering with a State of Iowa Registered Mechanical Engineer, computerized load calculations, custom sheet metal fabrication to SMACNA standards, and design-build work for offices, medical facilities, schools, hotels, warehouses and manufacturing.
