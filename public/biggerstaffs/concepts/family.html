Boilers

If your home heats with a boiler, we keep it running. Most boilers last 15 to 25 years. We maintain, repair and replace them with properly sized equipment.

Who is Biggerstaff Plumbing & Heating?

Biggerstaff Plumbing & Heating, Inc. is a family-owned plumbing, heating and air conditioning company in Lincoln, Nebraska. Keith Biggerstaff founded it in 1957 with his wife, June, working from their garage in the Havelock area of Lincoln. Their sons Doug, Dennis and Don Biggerstaff own it today.

Where is Biggerstaff located and what areas does it serve?

Biggerstaff is at 3605 N 40th Street, Lincoln, NE 68504. It serves all of Lincoln and surrounding areas such as Waverly, Eagle, Raymond, Denton, Roca and Bennet.

What are Biggerstaff's hours?

The office is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and closed Saturday and Sunday. Emergency service is available evenings and weekends. Call (402) 466-8118.

What does a service call cost?

Biggerstaff charges a $35.00 trip charge anywhere in Lincoln plus $120.00 per hour during regular business hours. Outside Lincoln there is an added $0.50 per mile.

What plumbing services does Biggerstaff offer?

Drain cleaning, sewer repair and camera inspection, leak detection, water heaters, sump pumps, water softeners and filtration, gas lines, bathroom and kitchen plumbing, hose bibs, yard hydrants, disposals, and backflow testing reported to the City of Lincoln, for homes and commercial buildings.

What heating and cooling services does Biggerstaff offer?

Furnace, heat pump, air conditioner and boiler repair, maintenance, installation and replacement, plus humidifiers, dehumidifiers, ductless mini splits and programmable thermostats. Biggerstaff is a Lennox Premier Dealer and a Mitsubishi Electric Diamond Contractor with NATE certified technicians.

Does Biggerstaff offer a maintenance plan?

Yes. The Service Contract includes two visits a year, air conditioning in the spring and furnace in the fall, plus a discount on parts and labor, for residential and commercial customers.

Does Biggerstaff offer financing?

Yes. Biggerstaff offers financing solutions; contact the office at (402) 466-8118 for details.

Does Biggerstaff work on mobile homes or RVs?

No. Biggerstaff does not specialize in work on RVs or mobile homes.
