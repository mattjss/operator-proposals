06Since 1942

Four names. Always in Celina.

Three generations of the Riesen family, led today by president Craig Riesen.

  1. 1942

    Fast Plumbing & Heating

    Earl and Elma Fast open in Celina. The early shop sat at 121 W. Logan St., an old hotel building with a barn in back.

  2. 1960

    Riesen Plumbing & Heating

    Forrest “Sam” Riesen takes over. From about 1969 the shop is on North Vine St.

    Riesen Bros. Plumbing, Heating & Cooling

    Craig and Lee Riesen run the business.

07Questions

Straight answers.

Anything else, call the office at (419) 586-2639.

Where is Riesen Plumbing & Heating located?

Riesen Plumbing & Heating Inc. is at 2015 State Rte 703, Celina, Ohio 45822. Call (419) 586-2639.

How long has Riesen been in business?

Since 1942. The company started in Celina as Fast Plumbing & Heating, run by Earl and Elma Fast, and has been led by three generations of the Riesen family. Craig Riesen is president.

What are Riesen's hours?

Monday through Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Saturday by appointment.

What services does Riesen offer?

Heating and air conditioning, plumbing and piping, and commercial and industrial work: furnaces, air conditioners and heat pumps, steam and hot water boilers, duct design and sheet metal fabrication, humidity control and indoor air treatment, water heaters, water lines, gas lines, sewer rodding, sewer camera inspection and jetting, water softeners, well pumps, sump pumps, backflow testing and fire sprinklers.

Is Riesen licensed?

Yes. Riesen holds State of Ohio plumbing, heating and hydronic licenses, a state water well contractor license, state and county septic system licenses and a fire sprinkler contractor license, and is licensed in Celina, St. Marys and Lima.

What furnace brand does Riesen sell?

Riesen is a factory authorized Tempstar dealer for furnaces, air conditioners and heat pumps, and the area's only Tempstar Elite Dealer. It services all makes and models and stocks parts for most.

Does Riesen do commercial and industrial work?

Yes. Riesen bids from blueprints and specifications, prepares design-build proposals, and has completed contracts for schools, water treatment plants, hospitals, churches, restaurants and manufacturers across west central Ohio, with evening and weekend service to reduce downtime.

Does Riesen offer financing?

Yes. Financing with low payments and no pre-pay penalty is available through an online application for furnaces, air conditioning and heat pumps, bath and kitchen remodels, and sewer or water repairs.

Does Riesen give free estimates?

Yes. Riesen offers free estimates for leaking basements, and you can request a free quote through its website.
