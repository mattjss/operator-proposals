What we do

Heating and air, done the long way.

The quick way is to swap the old box for a new one the same size. We measure, design and build the parts that decide how your house actually feels.

Have something else? We service all makes and models of heating and air equipment, and stock parts for most of them.

Heat loss and gain, by computer

We figure how much heat your house loses in winter and gains in summer, room by room, then size the furnace, AC or heat pump to match. Bigger is not better.

"Don't buy oversized equipment."

Duct design and sheet metal

Ducts sized for the air each room needs, so the system is quiet and can be balanced. We fabricate sheet metal in our own shop, plus PVC duct fabrication and welding.

"You draw it and we will build it."

Zoning

Separate control for upstairs and down, so the bedrooms get cooled even when the living room is already comfortable.

"Zoning is the only way to really cool the upstairs bedrooms."

Indoor air treatment

Help for allergy concerns, built into the system that already moves your air.

"We really can clean the air."

Humidity control

The right moisture in the air makes a house comfortable at a sensible thermostat setting, in summer and in winter.

"Humidity control for real comfort, summer and winter."

Steam and hot water boilers

We install and service steam and hot water boilers for homes, churches and businesses.
