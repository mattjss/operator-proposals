Chapter three

Sewer and trenchless

A video inspection first, to find the problem. Then the right repair: a full replacement, or a no-dig method that leaves most of the yard alone.

  • Sewer repair and replacementPipe repair, replacement and relocation, using specialized equipment "to help avoid leaks while ensuring your new pipes maintain proper water pressure".
  • No-dig trenchless repairNew polyethylene pipe or a fiberglass liner, without a trench across the yard.
  • Pipe bursting and pipe liningPipe lining uses a custom liner, inflated to seal the old pipe.
  • Drain cleaning and hydro jettingA pressurized stream of water clears grease, oil, hair and other debris.

FAQ

Questions before you sign.

Anything else, call (206) 938-3219. Phones are answered 24/7.

Where is Puget Sound Plumbing & Heating located?

Puget Sound Plumbing & Heating is a family-owned, locally operated plumbing and heating company at 11803 Des Moines Memorial Dr S in Burien, Washington. It serves Seattle and the greater Puget Sound region across King, Pierce and Snohomish counties.

Is Puget Sound Plumbing & Heating available 24/7?

Yes. Phones are answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, at (206) 938-3219. There is no extra charge for after-hours, weekend or holiday service calls.

How quickly can a plumber arrive in an emergency?

Puget Sound Plumbing & Heating aims for same-day service and often arrives within one to two hours of a call, depending on the day and demand. You get an ETA when you call.

Is Puget Sound Plumbing & Heating licensed?

Yes. The company is licensed, bonded and insured in Washington State, with General Contractor license PUGETSP929CF and Plumbing license PUGETSP791OB, both verifiable with Washington L&I. It is a BBB accredited business.

How long has Puget Sound Plumbing & Heating been in business?

Puget Sound Plumbing & Heating has served homeowners and businesses in the Seattle and Burien area for more than 20 years. It is locally owned, family operated and not a franchise.

What services does Puget Sound Plumbing & Heating offer?

Plumbing repair, installation and maintenance; drain cleaning and hydro jetting; sewer repair and replacement; no-dig trenchless sewer repair, pipe bursting, pipe lining and leak detection; repiping; water heaters including tankless and heat pump models; water filtration; and heating and cooling, including furnaces, boilers, heat pumps and air conditioning.

What warranties does Puget Sound Plumbing & Heating offer?

A lifetime warranty on water heater parts and labor (10 years on the tank), lifetime warranties on copper water services, complete water and waste repipes, and sewer replacements with schedule 40 PVC pipe, and a 20-year warranty on PEX (Wirsbo) water services.

How does pricing work?

Puget Sound Plumbing & Heating uses flat-rate, upfront pricing and gives a written, itemized quote before any work starts. It accepts all major credit cards and offers financing through Hearth and Wells Fargo for larger jobs.

Does Puget Sound Plumbing & Heating work on commercial properties?

Yes. It serves both residential and commercial clients throughout the Puget Sound region, including plumbing, water heaters, drain and sewer work, and heating and cooling.
