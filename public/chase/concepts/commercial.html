01Capabilities

Mechanical systems we install, repair and maintain

One contractor for the heating, cooling, plumbing and water equipment in your building. Grouped by system, so you can find what's on your roof or in your mechanical room.

AHeating and cooling

Commercial heating and air conditioning for all major brands, from routine tune-ups to repairs and replacement.

  • Rooftop packaged units

    Service, repair and replacement of the packaged units on commercial roofs.

  • Commercial heating

    Furnace repair and installation, energy-efficient models and routine tune-ups.

  • Commercial air conditioning

    Installation and repair, including refrigerant leaks, drain clogs and coil cleaning.

  • Boilers

    Boiler installation and service, especially in older buildings.

  • Heat pumps and mini-splits

    Ductless mini-splits and central split systems, an energy-efficient alternative to traditional heating.

  • Zoned heating and cooling

    Zoned systems for buildings where different areas need different temperatures.

  • Thermostats

    Smart thermostat installation, plus thermostat repair and replacement.

  • Indoor air quality systems

    Air quality systems installed alongside your heating and cooling.

BPlumbing and gas

Commercial plumbing done correctly the first time, from fixtures and water heaters to the sewer and gas lines.

  • Water heaters

    Tank and tankless repair and installation, with recirculation pumps.

  • Gas lines

    Gas pipe and gas line repair for heating equipment and kitchens.

  • Sewer lines and drains

    Sewer line repair, drain clearing and root removal.

  • Camera scope

    Camera inspection of drain and sewer lines to find the problem.

  • Leak detection and repair

    Finding and fixing leaks in supply and drain lines.

  • Fixtures and restrooms

    Faucets, fixtures, toilets and garbage disposals installed and repaired.

  • Repiping

    Repiping in copper and PEX.

  • Septic installs and repairs

    Septic installation and repair, including septic lines.

    To confirm: septic service details

CWater treatment

Testing first, then the right system for your water, whether it comes from the city or a well.

  • Water testing and analysis

    Testing for city or well water before recommending a system.

  • Whole-house filtration

    Filtration for all of the water entering the building.

  • Point-of-use filters

    Under-sink filters where you need them.

  • Reverse osmosis

    Removes up to 99% of dissolved solids, including lead, arsenic and fluoride.

  • Water softeners

    Salt-based and salt-free softeners, including NorthStar and the Nuvo Trio.

    10FAQ

    Common questions

    Anything else, call 505-396-2693.

    Who is Chase Mechanical?

    Chase Mechanical is a locally owned and operated heating, cooling, plumbing and water quality company in Albuquerque, New Mexico. It has served home and business owners since 2021.

    Where does Chase Mechanical work?

    Chase Mechanical serves Albuquerque, Santa Fe, Rio Rancho, Peralta, Belen, Los Lunas, Placitas and Bernalillo, New Mexico.

    Is Chase Mechanical licensed and insured?

    Yes. Chase Mechanical states that it is licensed, bonded and insured, and its work is done by licensed and insured technicians.

    What are Chase Mechanical's hours?

    The office is open Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 3:30 PM. Call 505-396-2693 or email adrian@chaseabq.com.

    What services does Chase Mechanical offer?

    Furnace, boiler and heat pump installation and repair; air conditioning, mini-split, zoned cooling and swamp cooler service; plumbing repairs, water heaters, sewer lines, drain clearing, camera scope and gas lines; water testing, filtration, reverse osmosis and water softeners; and septic installs and repairs.

    Does Chase Mechanical work on commercial buildings?

    Yes. Chase Mechanical serves residential and commercial customers, including restaurants, offices, retail spaces and warehouses, with rooftop packaged units, boilers and service plans.

    What warranty does Chase Mechanical offer?

    Chase Mechanical provides a 1-year labor warranty and 10-year parts coverage on new HVAC installations.

    Does Chase Mechanical offer discounts or financing?

    Yes. Chase Mechanical offers 5% off for military, seniors and first responders, and financing is available. Estimates are free and no-obligation.

    Which water softeners does Chase Mechanical install?

    Chase Mechanical installs salt-based and salt-free water softeners, including NorthStar and the saltless Nuvo Trio, as well as whole-house filters and reverse osmosis systems.
