Start with the problem

What homeowners call us to fix

Most upgrades start with something that bothers you every day. Find yours, read how we handle it, then book a visit so we can look at your home.

  1. 01

    One room is always too hot or too cold

    A hot upstairs or a cold back bedroom often needs more than a bigger unit. Zoned heating and cooling or a ductless mini-split can treat that space on its own.

    Zoning and mini-splits
  2. 02

    Your swamp cooler can't keep up

    Swamp coolers remain a popular, cost-effective choice here. We service and install them, and we install refrigerated air and heat pumps when you want to switch.

    Compare cooling
  3. 03

    Your furnace is getting old

    Before you replace like for like, ask about a heat pump: an energy-efficient alternative to traditional heating that also cools in summer.

    Heating options
  4. 04

    Spots, scale and hard water

    We test your water first, then recommend the right fix: a salt-based or salt-free softener, whole-house filtration, or both.

    Softeners and filters
  5. 05

    You're not sure about your well water

    We test city and well water. Reverse osmosis can remove up to 99% of dissolved solids, including lead, arsenic and fluoride.

    Water testing

Home comfort

Heating and cooling sized to how you live in the house

In Albuquerque's high desert climate, dependable cooling is a necessity, and winter nights still call for heat you can count on. We work on all major brands and install new systems with 10-year parts coverage.

Heat pumps and mini-splits

Ductless mini-splits and central split systems. An energy-efficient way to heat and cool, room by room or whole house.

Zoned heating and cooling

Separate control for the rooms that run hot or cold, so you stop conditioning the whole house to fix one floor.

Swamp coolers and refrigerated air

Swamp cooler service and installation, air conditioner installation and repair, and AC tune-ups for single-family homes, condos and manufactured homes.

Furnaces and boilers

Furnace repair and installation, including energy-efficient models, plus boiler installation and service, routine tune-ups and gas line repairs.

Smart thermostats

Thermostat installation and repair, including smart thermostats that work with your new system.

Questions

Common questions

Something else? Call 505-396-2693.

Who is Chase Mechanical?

Chase Mechanical is a locally owned and operated heating, cooling, plumbing and water quality company in Albuquerque, New Mexico. It has served home and business owners since 2021.

Where does Chase Mechanical work?

Chase Mechanical serves Albuquerque, Santa Fe, Rio Rancho, Peralta, Belen, Los Lunas, Placitas and Bernalillo, New Mexico.

Is Chase Mechanical licensed and insured?

Yes. Chase Mechanical states that it is licensed, bonded and insured, and its work is done by licensed and insured technicians.

What are Chase Mechanical's hours?

The office is open Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 3:30 PM. Call 505-396-2693 or email adrian@chaseabq.com.

What services does Chase Mechanical offer?

Furnace, boiler and heat pump installation and repair; air conditioning, mini-split, zoned cooling and swamp cooler service; plumbing repairs, water heaters, sewer lines, drain clearing, camera scope and gas lines; water testing, filtration, reverse osmosis and water softeners; and septic installs and repairs.

Does Chase Mechanical work on commercial buildings?

Yes. Chase Mechanical serves residential and commercial customers, including restaurants, offices, retail spaces and warehouses, with rooftop packaged units, boilers and service plans.

What warranty does Chase Mechanical offer?

Chase Mechanical provides a 1-year labor warranty and 10-year parts coverage on new HVAC installations.

Does Chase Mechanical offer discounts or financing?

Yes. Chase Mechanical offers 5% off for military, seniors and first responders, and financing is available. Estimates are free and no-obligation.

Which water softeners does Chase Mechanical install?

Chase Mechanical installs salt-based and salt-free water softeners, including NorthStar and the saltless Nuvo Trio, as well as whole-house filters and reverse osmosis systems.
