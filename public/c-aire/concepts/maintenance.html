Why a PMA

Find the problem on a check-up, not on a Saturday in July

On the Grand Strand, cooling season is long and the summer rush is when equipment works hardest. A preventative maintenance agreement (PMA) puts your systems on a schedule, so problems get found on a check-up instead of on a Saturday night.

  1. 01

    Priority service when something breaks

    Agreement customers get priority service on repair calls, for home systems and commercial equipment alike.

  2. 02

    Everything on one agreement

    AC, heat pumps, package units, mini splits, ice and soft serve machines, walk-ins, reach-ins and kitchen air. One company, one schedule, one call.

  3. 03

    Manufacturer warranty support

    C-Aire handles manufacturer warranty support, so covered parts go through the right channel.

  4. 04

    Check-ups on a set schedule

    Visits are planned around your equipment and your season, before the heat arrives.

    What a check-up covers

    A technician works through every unit on the agreement

    The point of a check-up is to find what's wearing out while it's still a part, not a weekend of lost product. You get a report of what needs attention.

    To confirm: check-up list
    • Inspect the heat pump, air conditioning and heating
    • Check pressures and refrigerant levels
    • Check wiring and coils
    • Report issues such as fan motors or compressors drawing too many amps

    Where is C-Aire located?

    C-Aire is a family-owned HVAC and commercial refrigeration company at 2607 Allen Dew Rd in Conway, South Carolina. Call 843-397-6568.

    How long has C-Aire served the Grand Strand?

    C-Aire has served the Grand Strand for over 20 years.

    What areas does C-Aire serve?

    C-Aire serves Horry, Georgetown, Florence and Sumter counties, including Conway, Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Carolina Forest, Surfside Beach, Socastee, Murrells Inlet, Pawleys Island, Georgetown, Florence and Sumter.

    Does C-Aire work on homes and businesses?

    Yes. C-Aire provides residential and commercial HVAC service, and commercial refrigeration for restaurants and businesses.

    What HVAC services does C-Aire offer?

    C-Aire installs and repairs air conditioning, repairs heat pumps and heating, services commercial exhaust hoods and make-up air units for restaurants, and works on heat pumps, package units and mini splits.

    Does C-Aire repair ice machines?

    Yes. C-Aire services and repairs commercial ice machines and soft serve ice machines.

    What commercial refrigeration does C-Aire service?

    C-Aire installs and repairs walk-in coolers and services reach-in coolers, prep tables, bar and back-bar refrigeration, under-counter units, coffin cases for grocery and convenience stores, and air curtains.

    Does C-Aire offer maintenance contracts?

    Yes. C-Aire offers preventative maintenance agreements (PMA) that include priority service.
