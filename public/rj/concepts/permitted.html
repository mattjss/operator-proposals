08 · FAQ

Questions, answered on the record.

Not here? Call (970) 420-6528.

Who owns RJ Plumbing & Heating?

RJ Plumbing & Heating is owned by Ray Van Sickle, a licensed Master Plumber. RJ is short for Raymond John. The company is family owned and operated in Fort Collins, Colorado.

Is RJ Plumbing & Heating the same company as R&J Plumbing?

Yes. R&J Plumbing has grown into RJ Plumbing & Heating. It is the same local team with a new name and logo.

Where does RJ Plumbing & Heating work?

RJ Plumbing & Heating serves Fort Collins and Northern Colorado, including Loveland, Windsor, Greeley, Berthoud, Wellington, Johnstown, Timnath, Evans, Livermore and Red Feather Lakes.

Is RJ Plumbing & Heating licensed and insured?

Yes. RJ Plumbing & Heating is fully licensed and insured, follows local codes, and obtains permits when required.

What plumbing services does RJ Plumbing & Heating offer?

New construction plumbing, plumbing for bathroom and kitchen remodels and additions, service calls and repairs, tank and tankless water heater installation and service, gas lines, and Nelson horse waterers.

Does RJ Plumbing & Heating work on boilers and furnaces?

Yes. RJ Plumbing & Heating installs, replaces and services boilers, including gas and hydronic heating systems, installs and repairs furnaces, and handles no-heat service calls.

Are estimates free?

Yes. RJ Plumbing & Heating provides free estimates for most installations and larger projects. Permit fees are typically not included unless noted.

How do I request service from RJ Plumbing & Heating?

Call (970) 420-6528, use the contact form to schedule an appointment, or use the online quote request form, which accepts up to three photos of the job.

Does a plumbing project in Fort Collins need a permit?

Some do. Permits are typically required when equipment such as a water heater is installed or replaced or when plumbing systems are modified, as enforced by the City of Fort Collins Building Services Department. Routine repairs generally do not require permits.
