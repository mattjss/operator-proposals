09 · FAQ

Questions owners and facility managers ask

Anything else, call the office at 717-796-9882.

Who owns H.L. Stoneberger Plumbing & Heating?

H.L. Stoneberger Plumbing & Heating, Inc. is a family owned and operated company in Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania, founded in 1986 by master plumber Harold Stoneberger and his wife Sharon. It is not a national chain or franchise.

Where is H.L. Stoneberger located?

The office is at 939 Nixon Dr., Mechanicsburg, PA 17055. Call the office at 717-796-9882.

What areas does H.L. Stoneberger serve?

H.L. Stoneberger serves Central Pennsylvania, including Mechanicsburg, Camp Hill, Harrisburg, Linglestown, Middletown, Carlisle, Hershey, Lewisberry, Dillsburg, Etters, Dover, Boiling Springs, Enola, Grantham, Lemoyne, Mount Holly Springs, New Cumberland and surrounding areas.

Does H.L. Stoneberger offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes. H.L. Stoneberger answers plumbing, no-heat and no-A/C emergencies 24 hours a day, seven days a week, including holidays, throughout its service area, for problems such as sewage backups and burst pipes.

What services does H.L. Stoneberger provide?

Plumbing, heating and air conditioning: plumbing repairs and installations, kitchen and bathroom plumbing remodels, sewer lateral repair, installation and inspection, water heaters, sump pumps, furnaces, boilers, heat pumps, A/C installation and repair, and regular HVAC maintenance.

Does H.L. Stoneberger do commercial work?

Yes. H.L. Stoneberger handles commercial plumbing and HVAC from small retail spaces to large manufacturing facilities, including underground plumbing with site excavation and saw cutting, rooftop piping, building ventilation and backflow testing. Past commercial work includes Verizon Wireless stores, the KTR warehouse in Carlisle, bathroom fixtures at Hotel Hershey and the Patient First clinic in Harrisburg.

What water heaters does H.L. Stoneberger install?

H.L. Stoneberger installs electric, gas, tankless, heat pump, point-of-use and solar water heaters, with brands including A.O. Smith, Noritz and Navien.

Is H.L. Stoneberger licensed?

Yes. H.L. Stoneberger lists PA Home Improvement Contractor registration HICR #PA031419, along with MDHVACR# 109590, FLRM 14017011 and FLRF 10067668.

How do I get a quote from H.L. Stoneberger?

Call 717-796-9882 for a free quote, or use the Request A Quote or Request Service Appointment form on the website.
